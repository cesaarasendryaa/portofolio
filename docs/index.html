/About Me

I love to work and be creative

Hi there! My name is Caesar Rasendrya, though I usually go by Caesar. a student who loves turning ideas into clean, easy-to-use designs. I start in Figma, planning layouts and interfaces, then bring them to life with HTML, CSS, and Tailwind CSS. For me, good design should feel simple and work well on every screen.

Outside the screen, I find balance in reading the Quran and studying, then recharge through football, running, equestrian, freediving, and hiking. These activities have taught me discipline, focus, patience, and the importance of staying calm under pressure. I carry the same mindset into every project I build, always looking to learn, improve, and grow.
